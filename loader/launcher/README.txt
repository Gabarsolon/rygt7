background.png is the picture behind the ryGT7 launcher's home view. To use your own, replace it with
any PNG of the same name (16:9 looks best); several (background.png, background2.png, ...) fade from one
to the next. Without one, the launcher uses GT7's own picture (pic1.png) from your game files.

logo.png is the ryGT7 logo shown in place of the title (on the home view and in the settings). Delete it
for the title in text.
